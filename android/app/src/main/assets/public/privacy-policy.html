Privacy Policy — Smart India Work

Package ID: in.smartindiawork.app • Effective Date: November 2026

Smart India Work connects local customers with skilled artisans and service workers across India. We respect your privacy and handle personal data transparently in compliance with applicable laws and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Profile & Account Information: Name, phone number, city, locality/address, and role (Customer or Worker).
  • Worker Verification Details: Trade/skill category, daily rate, optional masked Aadhaar last-4 digits for trust verification, and work portfolio photos uploaded by the worker.
  • Location Information: Approximate or precise locality/GPS coordinates solely when you choose to share service location or track an active booking.
  • Booking & Communication Data: Job posts, service booking statuses, and in-app messages between customer and assigned worker.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Customer ID or Worker ID.
  • To match customers with nearby verified workers and notify workers about booked jobs.
  • To enable communication, live job arrival tracking, and platform fee/ledger recordkeeping.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

Your profile, bookings, and job posts are stored securely over HTTPS/TLS using Google Firebase Firestore. We do not sell your personal information to third-party advertisers. Customer contact address details are shared with an assigned worker only after the worker accepts the booking.

4. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may reset local app data directly inside the app under Profile → Reset App Cache. To request complete deletion of your cloud-stored Customer ID, Worker ID, or booking records from Firebase Firestore, please email us at vr6386833@gmail.com with your registered mobile number and ID name. Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.
